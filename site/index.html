No gaps. No stale scores. Age ratings you can trust.

MetaMender works quietly in the background of Kodi. It fills in what your scraper left empty, keeps scores from freezing at their first-week number, and gives every movie and show an age rating from your own country.

Install MetaMender Documentation View on GitHub

Three things it fixes

Gaps get filled

Missing plots, episode titles, credits, taglines and more, from TMDB and TheTVDB, in each folder's own language, with an optional fallback language.

BeforeNo plot
After"The night crossing to the islands is delayed..."

Scores that keep up

Scrapers fetch a new episode's score once, when it has a handful of votes. MetaMender checks it again while the votes come in, then leaves it alone once it settles.

First week9.4 (12 votes)
Three months on7.8 (4,210 votes)

Age ratings you can trust

Movies and shows get their age rating in your own country's system, for ten countries, so you can decide what the kids may watch.

AfterNL:12
